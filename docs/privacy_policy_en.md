# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

That Nameless Game (the "App") respects the privacy of its users.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information.

The App does not transmit the following information to external servers:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location information
- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers
- Gameplay history
- In-app settings

## 2. Information Stored on the Device

The App stores the following information locally on the user's device:

- Display language
- BGM and sound effect settings
- Time limit settings
- Game achievements and progress

This information is used only to provide the App's functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## 3. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements.

The App does not use advertising services or advertising-related tracking technologies.

## 4. Analytics

The App does not use analytics services that transmit usage information to external servers.

## 5. Sharing of Information

The App does not sell, share, or provide user information to third parties.

## 6. Internet Communication

During normal use, the App does not transmit gameplay data or settings to external servers.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not collect or store personal information from children or other users.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality, applicable laws, or platform requirements change.

Any significant changes will be reflected on this page.
